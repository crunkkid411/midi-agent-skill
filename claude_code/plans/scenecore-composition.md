# Scenecore Composition Plan

## Brief

**Genre**: Scenecore (hyperpop subgenre)
**Mood**: Aggressive, glossy, nostalgic-maximal, hyper-energetic
**Key**: A Minor
**BPM**: 140

## Principles

### Principle 1: Hyperpop Chord Stabs
- Simple progression: A minor → F major (VI relative major creates tension)
- Extended 7th chords for gloss
- Sharp staccato attacks, short release

### Principle 2: Aggressive Trap/Breakcore Drums
- 140 BPM trap-inspired with fast hi-hat runs
- Loud 808 kick on every beat + offbeats
- Hard clap/snare, 16th-note hi-hat runs

### Principle 3: Distorted Maximum Texture
- Heavy-distorted 808 bass, tight LPF
- Bright supersaw synths with filter sweep
- OTT-compressed, loud mix
- High velocities (0.6-0.9)

## Song Structure (16-bar loop concept)

| Section | Bars | Content |
|---------|------|---------|
| Drop A | 8 | Full: drums + bass + synth stabs + arp |
| Variation | 8 | Add arpeggio, change rhythm |

## Strudel Stack Layers

1. **Drums** — Roland TR-808, kick/snare/hh
2. **Bass** — 808 sub A1, tight low-pass
3. **Synth Chords** — Am7 → F major supersaw stabs
4. **Arpeggio Lead** — Fast 16th pattern, bright lead

## Sound Targets

- Kick: "bd" from TR-808 bank
- Snare: "sd" or "clap" from TR-808
- Hi-hats: "hh" open/closed patterns
- Bass: 808-style with distortion/overdrive
- Synths: Superdough "supersaw" or "pluck" presets
